About Forestry Gyan

Growing knowledge for sustainable forests and exam-oriented learning 🌱

Founder • Forestry Gyan

Building Clarity in Forestry & Environment 🌿

Forestry Gyan was started with a simple vision — to make Forestry, Environment, and Ecology clear, accessible, and exam-oriented for aspirants.

With a strong focus on conceptual understanding, current relevance, and answer-writing orientation, the platform aims to support aspirants preparing for UPSC, IFoS, and State Forest Services.

🌿 Who We Are

Forestry Gyan is an independent learning initiative dedicated to students and aspirants preparing for UPSC, IFoS, State Forest Services, and other environment-related examinations.

We focus on delivering concept-based, exam-oriented, and current-affairs-integrated content related to forestry, environment, ecology, biodiversity, and conservation.

🎯 Our Mission

To make quality forestry and environmental education accessible, simplified, and freely available for every serious aspirant across India.

🌱 What We Offer

  • ✔ Conceptual forestry & environment notes
  • ✔ Question of the Day (Prelims & Mains focused)
  • ✔ Current affairs related to environment & forestry
  • ✔ Infographics, PDFs, and visual learning tools
  • ✔ Exam-oriented explanations for UPSC & IFoS

🤝 Our Philosophy

We believe that strong fundamentals + regular revision + relevance to current affairs are the keys to cracking competitive examinations.

Forestry Gyan aims to bridge the gap between academic forestry and exam-oriented application.
